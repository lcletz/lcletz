🧮 **New nonparametric estimation method** 🧮
As part of a 6-months internship during my second year of my master’s program, I had to familiarize myself with cure models in survival analysis and then develop a completely new estimator for the conditional hazard function in mixed cure models. This estimator relies exclusively on observed data (duration and event status) and is based on the projection theorem. It exhibits good mathematical properties and yields interpretable results. The plan is to follow up on this study with 3-years doctoral program in order to incorporate covariates into the model and create an R package that brings together all of the methods.

🏥 **A Clinical Trial: BICAR-ICU** 🏥
The first clinical study I worked on was the multicenter, randomized, controlled BICAR-ICU clinical trial, in which we were tasked with evaluating the efficacy of sodium bicarbonate in the treatment of severe metabolic acidosis in patients hospitalized in the intensive care unit. Due to missing data, we first tested various multiple imputation methods, then assessed the impact of the treatment on mortality and the need for renal replacement therapy using logistic regression, which showed that this treatment helped patients with a high renal insufficiency score survive longer and without requiring dialysis.

🐀 **Collaboration with Students from the Institut Agro** 🐀
During a few weeks, we collaborated with students from the Institut Agro to assist them with their statistics-related projects. A first small project involved evaluating the effectiveness of blobs as detectors of pollutants on wood; to do this, we had to calculate the minimum number of experiments needed to achieve sufficient statistical power. A second, more ambitious project involved determining the associations and interactions between intestinal parasites and digestive bacteria in the rat microbiome; to do this, we helped them identify these associations using correspondence analysis and a generalized linear model with mixed effects. Both projects were presented in detail on posters.

🚱 **Water contamination from former mine** 🚱
My last work during my first Master's degree year is a 2-months internship where the codes are available [here](https://github.com/lcletz/PollutionEauxCarnoules). I had to explore a physico-chemicals' database in order to determine correlations and interactions between rainfall and chemicals found in the water near a former mine in Gard, France. The results will allow HydroSciences Montpellier to get an idea of how many samples a year they will need to monitor efficiently the site.

🌿 **Conformal prediction and PlantNet** 🌿
The work that I'm really proud of is certainly this [project](https://github.com/lcletz/PLANTNET_M1_SSD)! We studied a huge database of the PlantNet mobile application to apply conformal prediction and thus improve the app by displaying only the smallest set of labels (species) with 95% confidence that the correct species is shown. I worked mainly on descriptive statistics, but also on calculating the majority vote and processing the 7 mn datas so that my colleagues could calculate the scores and try out different calibration methods. Important information: I had two wonderful and serious coworkers and two really supportive supervisors!
